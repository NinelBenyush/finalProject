## 🧭 Overview  

**OrderBoost** was created to solve a common business challenge — **inventory shortages and overstock**.  
For many business owners, especially those working with international suppliers, it’s difficult to predict how much stock to order due to:  

- Unpredictable shipment delays  
- Holidays or seasonal demand  
- Temporary product trends  
- Changes in customer preferences  

These issues lead to:  
- ❌ *Lost customers and poor reputation* (when items are out of stock)  
- 💸 *High storage costs and losses* (when inventory is overstocked)  

**OrderBoost** offers a **machine learning–based solution** that analyzes past sales and stock data to **predict future inventory needs**, helping business owners make **data-driven and cost-effective decisions**.

---

## 🌟 Key Benefits  

✅ Prevent inventory shortages and surpluses  
💰 Save storage and restocking costs  
🤝 Increase customer satisfaction and business reputation  
📊 Make smarter, data-driven decisions

## ⚙️ System Architecture  

### 🖥️ Frontend  
- **Vite + React** – For fast and responsive web UI development  
- **Tailwind CSS** – Clean, modern, and customizable design  

### 🧠 Backend  
- **Flask (Python)** – For communication between client and server  
- **PyTorch (LSTM Model)** – For demand forecasting and time series prediction  
- **Pandas** – Data loading, cleaning, and preprocessing  
- **NumPy** – Numerical operations and calculations  
- **Matplotlib** – Data visualization and graphs  
- **Scikit-learn** – Additional machine learning utilities  
- **JWT Authentication** – Secure user authentication and session management  

### 🗄️ Database  
- **SQLite** – Lightweight SQL database storing:  
  - User information (name, email, password)  
  - Prediction results and uploaded files
